Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I book a session?

Message us on Messenger, Viber or WhatsApp, or send the form below. We'll confirm availability, recommend a set, and reserve your slot.

When should I book a newborn session?

Ideally during pregnancy, so we can hold a date around your due date. Newborn portraits are best taken in baby's first days, and we'll plan the timing with you.

When is the best time for maternity photos?

Most mamas shoot in the late second to early third trimester, when the bump is beautifully round and you still feel comfortable. We'll advise based on your due date.

What should we wear?

Soft, simple tones photograph beautifully. After booking we'll share a styling guide, and we have gowns and wardrobe options for maternity sessions — ask us what's available.

How long is a session, and can we take breaks?

We work at your little one's pace. Breaks for feeding, changing and cuddles are always part of the session.

How and when do we receive our photos?

Your retouched images are delivered in a private online gallery. We'll share the expected turnaround when you book.

Is there a deposit, and can I reschedule?

A reservation fee holds your date. Rescheduling is possible with notice — especially for newborns and little ones who have their own plans. Ask us for the current policy.

Do you offer studio rentals and Christmas sessions?

Yes — both. Christmas sets are limited and released seasonally; early-bird slots are announced before the set reveal.

Where are you located?

South Green Park Village, Parañaque. We'll send exact directions and parking details when you book.
