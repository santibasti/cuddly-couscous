How to request deletion of your data

To have your information removed from TopMop Window Cleaning Solutions Corp. records:

  1. Send a message to our Facebook Page, TopMop Window Cleaning Solutions Corp., with the words "Delete my data" and the name you used with us.
  2. We will confirm your identity and delete your information, including any Facebook messages we kept, within 30 days. Records we must keep by law, such as invoices, may be kept for the legally required period.

See also our Privacy Policy.
