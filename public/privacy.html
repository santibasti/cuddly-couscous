Privacy Policy

TopMop Window Cleaning Solutions Corp. ("TopMop", "we") is a professional window, glass, solar panel and property-care cleaning company in the Philippines. This policy explains how we handle information in our business tools and on our Facebook Page.

What we collect

  • Customers and enquiries: name, contact details, site address and job details that you give us when you ask for a quotation or book a service.
  • Facebook Page messages: if you message our Facebook Page, we keep your message and your name as shown by Facebook so we can reply to your enquiry.
  • Job photos: photos taken at the work site. We use a photo in our marketing only if the client agreed, and we hide faces, house numbers and other identifying details where needed.

How we use it

To prepare quotations, schedule and carry out services, send service reminders and invoices, answer enquiries, and publish updates and tips on our Facebook Page. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers we need to run the business (for example hosting, messaging and payment providers), or when the law requires it.

Keeping it safe

Our records are stored with access controls, and only authorized TopMop staff can view them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time. See how to request deletion.

Contact

Message the TopMop Window Cleaning Solutions Corp. Facebook Page, or contact us using the details on our quotations and invoices.
